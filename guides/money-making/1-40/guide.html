What to do next

Start with surplus logs or fish and collect quest rewards when their goals overlap your training. Iron Platelegs are an early option once Mining 10 and Smithing 18 are ready. Silver Topaz Amulets give the highest base GP/h among the gathering and crafting chains compared here. Use a simpler gathering action if its rate with your tools beats the chain or if you want the materials for another skill.

For bait-free fish sales, upgrade your spot along this base GP/h sequence as the levels become available. Shallow Pools (1) → Calm Shallows (6) → Quiet Bend (12) → Dock Waters (18) → Pebble Bank (23) → Windswept Shores (27) → Pier Walk (32) → Rapid Runs (39). Keep raw fish you intend to cook for combat or Thieving.

For early Thieving, try Citizen or Thug first and check your food use. Locked Thug Chest at Thieving 10 takes either a Lockpick or a Thug Chest Key in the toolbelt. Thug can drop Lockpicks. Each chest attempt spends a pick regardless of success. The reusable key gives +500 Stealth and +50 Fortune on that chest. Compare chest runs with your actual food and pick supply, then try Fisherman at 20 or Bandit and Chef later in the range.

Worked chain for Iron Platelegs

Mine 4 Iron Ore at 4.665 s each. Smelt 4 Iron Bars at 2.5 s each. Finish one pair of Iron Platelegs in 10.0 s and sell for 157 GP. The chain takes 38.66 s on average, or 14,619 GP/h. With four bars already in the bank, the same craft adds 109 GP over selling the bars immediately.

Fire Wand is another chain to compare. Buy Bucket of Sand for 5 GP each, smelt Molten Glass and craft an Unpowered Orb. Mine Rune Essence for Fire and Mind Runes. The model subtracts the cost of the sand.

Calculation assumptions
  • Gathering and crafting use expected yields across long runs and sell the listed output at the in-game GP price.
  • Chains start with empty stock, and their GP/h includes all listed gathering and processing plus shop input costs. Skill training, shopping clicks, menus, and sale time are outside the estimate.
  • Fishing uses the ordinary catch and Junk rolls. The scenario omits bait, priority fish, and special catches. Mining and Woodcutting omit extra drops such as Geodes and Bird Nests.
  • Thieving models direct GP and common loot. It includes normal failed attempts and stuns. Rare loot, extra pickpockets, food, and lockpick replenishment time are outside the estimate.
  • Combat values use published base drops and assume all listed loot can be collected for sale. GP/h requires a timed session with the chosen gear and supplies.

Combat through level 30

Start with Golbin Forest to check your damage and food use. Test Giant Chicken in Corrupted Farmlands next, then compare stronger targets as your gear improves. Try Mangrove Strider only when a supervised run shows the setup can sustain it. Check enemy attacks, loadout, healing and loot collection before leaving a fight unattended.

Check what an upgrade will save over the hours you intend to use it. Copper Fishing Rod opens at Fishing 5 and costs 2,000 GP plus 25 Copper Bars. Fishing's first +20 bank slots unlock at level 20 for 5,000 GP. Buy slots when space is cutting sessions short. Keep quest items, spares for upgrades, and at least the food, ammo, runes and bones you plan to use.

Estimate payback as the cost in GP and materials divided by the extra GP/h. For example, a 2,000 GP upgrade that adds 500 GP/h takes 4 hours to recover its cash cost. Add any required farming or training time if you are starting from scratch.

Continue past Combat 30 or non-combat 40

  1. Keep your current reliable method as a benchmark. Note your next cash goal and skills close to a new unlock. Check the current public build and patch notes before copying sale prices, recipes or loot tables.
  2. When Fishing reaches 44, test Rocky Shores. Compare it with the current spot using your rod, catch mix and bait costs. For Mining and Smithing, check Adamantite Ore at Mining 45 and verify the full new bar recipe before switching. In combat, test a single newly unlocked target and record net GP/h, including respawn, food, ammunition and runes.
  3. Compare passive Farming and Ranching income on their calendar time and count the active time to plant, feed or harvest. Price seed, food and other inputs. Add the net passive income to the active method only if both can operate together.
  4. For another published range, follow the extension instructions in the repository. Add only verified IDs and requirements, recalculate both views, and rebuild the library index.
